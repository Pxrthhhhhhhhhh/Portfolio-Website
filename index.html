About Me

I'm a Business Administration student based in Mumbai, which might seem like a pivot from the visual work you see here. I don't just capture beautiful visuals; I understand the story behind them. For me, there's a certain magic in taking a creative thought, looking through a lens, and translating it into a viewable medium that actually connects with an audience. My toolkit goes beyond just a camera. I shoot on my Sony paired with a range of lenses, work with professional lighting to shape every frame, and use dedicated mics to get clean, quality audio. I'm equally comfortable doing product photography and dialling in the right lighting to make every detail stand out. In post, everything comes together through the Adobe Creative Suite. I look forward to building something great with you.
